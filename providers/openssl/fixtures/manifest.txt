The OpenSSL provider test fixture creates a local CA and two leaf certificates at runtime.

Hosts:
- one.foundation.test
- two.foundation.test

The fixture binds only loopback and validates the CA bundle, both SNI selections,
an unknown SNI rejection, a hostname mismatch, and plaintext rejection by the TLS listener.
The native framing fixture also rejects malformed status lines, more than eight informational
responses, conflicting Content-Length and Transfer-Encoding fields, duplicate Content-Length,
malformed chunks, forbidden trailer framing fields, and headers above 64 KiB.
